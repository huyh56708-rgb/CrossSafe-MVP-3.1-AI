ROADMAP

Why Hybrid is the next step

Rule-based→Machine Learning→Hybrid

Rules are transparent and auditable, but can miss new wording.

ML / AI can detect patterns beyond exact phrases, but needs quality data and validation.

Hybrid combines explicit safety rules with broader pattern recognition while keeping critical warnings explainable.
